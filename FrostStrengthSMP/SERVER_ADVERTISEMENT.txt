📜 SERVER DESCRIPTION
================================================================================

Welcome to Frost SMP — where every kill makes you stronger and every death
makes you weaker. This is a high-stakes, kill-to-power Survival Multiplayer
server built for competitive PvP players who want progression, prestige, and
pure Minecraft combat.

Start at Strength +1. Kill a player → gain +1 Strength (more attack damage).
Die to a player → lose −1 Strength. Climb to the top, become the strongest
on the server, and claim The Crown.

But be careful: drop to −5 Strength and you're LOCKED — forced into spectator
until an admin pardons you, bans you, or you use a rare Revive Token.

================================================================================
⚔️ CORE MECHANICS
================================================================================

•  Strength = Damage — Every level of Strength adds +1 attack damage.
•  Persistent Progression — Strength saves across death, respawn, and relogs.
•  Killstreaks — Hit 3, 5, 10+ killstreaks for announcements, fireworks, and
  free Strength Bottles.
•  Weapon Classes — 9 classes (Sword, Axe, Bow, Crossbow, Trident, Mace,
  Spear, Shield, Armor). Each has a Passive (+2) and Ultimate (+4).
•  The Crown — Wear it for max Strength + 20 hearts. Take it off to restore
  your real strength.
•  Revive Token — Expensive, custom-designed item to escape the −5 lock.
•  Roles — Member, VIP, MVP, Strength God, Admin with perks and prefixes.
•  EZ Items — /ez gaps, anvil, shulker, webs for quality-of-life.
•  Custom Spawn — Beautiful particle/firework spawn effect on first join.
•  Built-in Anti-Cheat — Reach, speed, fly, fast-break, auto-clicker,
  kill-aura protection (Grim-style).
•  Mace Lock — Maces cannot be enchanted.

================================================================================
🎁 CUSTOM ITEMS & CRAFTING
================================================================================
